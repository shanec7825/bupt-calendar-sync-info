BUPT Calendar Sync

BUPT Calendar Sync is an independent, personal automation that copies a student's timetable from the BUPT mobile academic system and homework information from BUPT UCloud into Google Calendar and Google Tasks. It is not an official product of Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications or Google.

The automation runs through the owner's GitHub Actions configuration. It creates or updates course events, homework-deadline events, and homework tasks. It is designed for personal use and is not a public sign-up service.

Google account access

The application requests permission to manage Google Calendar and Google Tasks data. These permissions are broader than the particular calendars and task list the current sync is intended to maintain. The program reads the account's calendar and task-list metadata to locate its targets, then creates or updates matching events and tasks.

Read the Privacy Policy for the data processed, storage, and steps to revoke access.
